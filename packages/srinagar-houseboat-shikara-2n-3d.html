Kashmir Houseboat & Shikara Experience

Srinagar Only  ·  2 Nights / 3 Days

Overview

Sometimes all you want is the Dal Lake experience itself — waking up to the sound of water against a wooden hull, floating markets at dawn, and a slow shikara ride at sunset. This compact package is built purely around that, and works equally well as a standalone weekend trip or as an add-on before/after another itinerary you're already booking with us or elsewhere.

Trip Highlights

  • 2 nights aboard a traditional Kashmiri houseboat
  • Sunrise floating market shikara ride (optional, early start)
  • Sunset shikara ride included
  • Half-day Srinagar city and Mughal Garden tour
  • Flexible add-on to any longer Kashmir itinerary

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & Houseboat Check-in

Airport pickup, houseboat check-in, welcome tea, and an evening shikara ride on Dal Lake.

Day 2

City & Gardens

Half-day visit to Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh, with the afternoon free to relax on the houseboat deck or explore the old city.

Day 3

Departure

Optional early-morning floating market shikara ride, then transfer to the airport.

Inclusions & Exclusions

What's Included

  • 2 nights houseboat accommodation, double sharing
  • Daily breakfast
  • Airport transfers
  • 2 shikara rides (sunset + optional sunrise)

What's Not Included

  • Airfare/train fare
  • Lunch and dinner
  • Garden entry tickets
  • GST and anything not mentioned under inclusions
